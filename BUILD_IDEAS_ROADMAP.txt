1. THE 4 SCALE PROGRESSION TIERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 1: STARTER / COMPACT (Footprint: 10x10 to 20x20 blocks | 500 - 3,000 blocks)
  - Purpose: Day 1-15 survival homes, small utility workshops, compact outposts.
  - Target audience: New survival worlds, resource-limited players, realm starters.

TIER 2: MEDIUM / ESTATE / HAMLET (Footprint: 20x20 to 40x40 blocks | 3,000 - 15,000 blocks)
  - Purpose: Fully furnished 2-3 story residences, working farms, guild halls.
  - Target audience: Mid-game survivalists, server towns, cozy builders.

TIER 3: LARGE / MANOR / COMPLEX (Footprint: 40x40 to 70x70 blocks | 15,000 - 50,000 blocks)
  - Purpose: Multi-wing castles, luxury estates, industrial production centers.
  - Target audience: End-game bases, server hubs, centerpiece attractions.

TIER 4: MEGABUILDS & WONDERS (Footprint: 70x70+ blocks | 50,000 to 500,000+ blocks)
  - Purpose: Colossal cathedrals, under-mountain kingdoms, fantasy island empires.
  - Target audience: Showcase projects, massive server lobbies, adventure maps.

3. QUALITY CONTROL & VERIFICATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Foundation Rule: Always a 3-layer weathered stone/andesite gradient at base.
2. Framing Rule: Stripped log structural corners with horizontal floor dividers.
3. Contrast Rule: Roof trim must contrast with roof infill and overhang 1 block.
4. Interior Rule: Every room must have storytelling furnishings (kitchen, beds,
   candles, carpets, storage, workbenches). Never leave an empty box.
5. In-Game Testing: Every build MUST be loaded into Minecraft, inspected in-game,
   and photographed with real in-game screenshots before adding to MineVault.
